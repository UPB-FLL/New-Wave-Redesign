# South Florida search and AI-search baseline — New Wave IT — 2026-11-12

The state before the South Florida SEO change (PR #190), so later readings
have something to be compared with. Read-only: nothing on the site, in the
CMS, the database or any account was changed to produce it.

How it was measured: nine research agents and a critic, 2026-11-12 between
04:15 and 05:45 UTC, through the owner's Composio connections (Google Keyword
Planner, Ahrefs, SearchApi and SerpApi for localized Google results and
Google AI Mode, Firecrawl) and plain GETs of the live site. Marks:
**[checked]** = re-run or re-read by the session's orchestrator;
**[reported]** = taken from an agent's tool output, not re-run. One reading
on one night; generated answers and result pages vary between runs.

The scorer drops any heading with fewer than 20 words under it, and these
pages are a heading plus one 9 to 16-word sentence per card, so the price
cards and the office card are never scored. No page has a question heading
outside its closing call to action, and none has an FAQ.

## 5. What ranks and what is cited

- Page one of the local searches: 46% provider pages built for a city or a
  service, 31% directories (Clutch first on three Broward searches), the rest
  job boards, Facebook answer pages and provider blog posts. One homepage in
  72 results. 29 of 31 ranking provider titles name the place.
- Ten ranking city pages: 287 to 2,484 words, median about 1,100; half have
  an FAQ; one shows a street address in that city, one shows hours, none
  prints a price.
- Ten pages cited by AI Mode: nine have an FAQ, seven carry FAQPage schema,
  eight name the place asked about, and every page cited for a price prompt
  states a price with its unit.
