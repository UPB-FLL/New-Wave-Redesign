# GroundSwell process artwork

The five process illustrations are original dimensional artwork generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. They use the supplied GroundSwell navy, cyan, amber, and white palette, with a shared topographic platform, three-quarter camera, glass and ceramic materials, and studio lighting. They are illustrations of the process, not screenshots or measured results. The supplied logo masters remain the identity source.

Production assets are in `public/brand/social-engineering/07_Product_Illustrations/`. Each subject has a 640px and 1080px WebP with transparent alpha, preserving contact shadows. The step uses responsive sources and lazy loading; explicit dimensions reserve the space. Both sizes together total about 900KB across all five subjects. The 640px versions are 46–62KB each and the 1080px versions 105–147KB each; source selection depends on screen size and pixel density.

## Generation prompt set

Common direction: “Create one standalone premium 3D marketing illustration, not a website mockup or collage. Use a close three-quarter isometric product camera, matte midnight navy architectural ceramic, smoky cyan glass, crisp beveled edges, subtle amber details, and a navy topographic platform. Use softbox lighting, realistic contact shadows, and a clear layered silhouette filling about 80% of the composition. Palette: #09131D, #101E2D, #31C6CF, #F7FAFB, #F2A33A. Fully transparent background with actual alpha and preserved contact shadows. No readable text, labels, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI cards, AI brains, robots, purple, or flat vector diagrams.”

- Queue: “A miniature small-business storefront, with cyan glass windows and a restrained amber door detail. A large translucent cyan location pin settles over its roof. Two smaller business volumes sit farther back and connect to it by a fine cyan physical route track.”
- Research: “A substantial cyan-rimmed magnifying lens with a navy handle hovers over a survey platform. A globe, location marker, interlocking speech bubbles, and folded webpage specimen feed cyan glass tracks toward the inspected center. An amber point identifies the evidence under scrutiny.”
- Model: “Three translucent glass routes begin together, branch smoothly, and end at different heights. The center route is cyan and the side routes smoke glass. A navy-and-amber calibration arm studies the central route. Communicate comparing outcomes before choosing, without graph axes or figures.”
- Build: “A thick white summary sheet, presentation deck, and bound full-audit booklet form a broad fan over a navy drafting platform. Use fine navy rules, cyan blocks, abstract unlabeled charts, real paper thickness, an amber paper clip, and a cyan registration guide.”
- Deliver: “A navy document tray receives three thick white reports at slightly different angles. A substantial translucent cyan closed padlock stands beside it. A small amber completion tab accents the top report. Use restrained unlabeled editorial marks without readable words or digits.”

Research, Model, Build, and Deliver used the Queue image as the style/material reference. Production conversion only resizes and encodes the output to WebP; it preserves the generated alpha and does not alter the scene.
