**Date:** August 12, 2026  
**Owner:** New Wave IT LLC  
**Website:** https://www.newwaveitfl.com

## Purpose

Create a complete, production-ready New Wave IT brand package that evolves the existing identity without discarding its recognition. The work will preserve the wave/current concept, the New Wave IT name, the established cyan-and-green signal, and the line **“Don’t get lost in the current”**, while replacing inconsistent logo geometry, typography, colors, templates, and one-off website treatments with one governed system.

The completed package must work across the existing Vite/React website, social channels, proposals, support communications, invoices, presentations, print materials, email signatures, and future campaigns. It must look like an established South Florida managed service provider rather than a generic technology template or an AI-generated cybersecurity brand.

## Success Criteria

The final system is successful when:

- Existing customers still recognize New Wave IT immediately.
- The identity feels more polished, proprietary, technical, and established.
- Every production asset is generated from one canonical logo and token source.
- Website, social, print, and document materials use the same colors, type hierarchy, spacing, and graphic language.
- The design communicates calm control, responsiveness, technical competence, and local accountability.
- Important copy and logo elements remain readable at real platform sizes and within crop-safe areas.
- Color combinations meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast requirements for their intended text sizes.
- Files are clearly named, organized, editable, and ready to use without rebuilding them in Canva.
- The package avoids beach clichés, stock shield marks, padlocks, hooded-hacker imagery, fake dashboards, excessive neon, gratuitous glass effects, and generic circuit-board backgrounds.

## Logo System

### Canonical symbol

The canonical icon is a three-current mark constructed from three horizontally flowing open strokes. The strokes share one visual rhythm but are not copied mechanically; their offsets create forward movement and prevent the symbol from reading as a generic Wi-Fi or water icon.

Construction rules:

- Master artwork uses a square `64 × 64` SVG viewBox.
- The visible mark stays inside an optical `48 × 48` field with at least `8` units of surrounding space.
- All strokes use identical weight, round caps, and round joins.
- The three lines maintain even optical separation at their closest points.
- The mark must remain recognizable in one color and at 16 pixels.
- The standard icon always uses three strokes.
- A separate two-stroke micro variant is permitted only at rendered sizes of 24 pixels or smaller, including the 16-pixel favicon. It is an optical-size variant, not an alternate brand mark.
- Do not place the symbol inside a shield, hexagon, padlock, circuit, globe, palm tree, or letterform.

### Wordmark

The wordmark is **NEW WAVE IT** set in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold with custom optical spacing. The vector master will convert the approved wordmark to outlines so exported logo files do not depend on a locally installed font.

The wordmark remains direct and compact. It will not use futuristic cuts, gradient-filled letters, outlines, bevels, or faux metallic effects.

### Approved lockups

The package will include:

1. Primary horizontal lockup: icon left, wordmark right
2. Primary horizontal lockup with heritage line
3. Compact stacked lockup: icon above wordmark
4. Compact stacked lockup with heritage line
5. Standalone icon
6. Standalone wordmark
7. One-color horizontal and stacked lockups
8. Reversed white lockups for dark backgrounds
9. App-icon and favicon optical variants

### Color behavior

- The preferred full-color icon uses a controlled Signal Cyan to Continuity Green gradient.
- The wordmark is Current Navy on light backgrounds and Cloud White on dark backgrounds.
- The heritage line uses Tide Blue on light backgrounds and Signal Cyan on dark backgrounds.
- One-color marks may use Current Navy, Cloud White, black, or a single approved spot-equivalent color.
- Gradients are never used in the wordmark itself.

### Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space equals the height of one wave stroke’s crest-to-trough amplitude, represented in the guide as `x`.
- Keep at least `x` around standalone icons and `1.5x` around complete lockups.
- Minimum digital width: `140 px` for the horizontal lockup without the heritage line.
- Minimum digital width: `220 px` for a lockup with the heritage line.
- Minimum print width: `1.25 in` without the heritage line and `2 in` with it.
- Below these sizes use the icon or wordmark separately rather than compressing the full lockup.

### Prohibited logo use

Do not:

- Redraw or manually approximate the wave paths
- Stretch, skew, rotate, crop, or rearrange the lockup
- Change individual wave colors
- Add glow, bevel, drop shadow, outline, or 3D depth to the master logo
- Put the full-color mark on a visually busy image without a controlled field
- Use the micro favicon variant as a large logo
- Recreate the wordmark with a substitute font
- Place additional copy inside the logo clear-space field

## Typography

### Primary display: Plus Jakarta Sans

Use for the wordmark source, page titles, campaign headlines, major headings, navigation, buttons, and concise data callouts.

Approved weights: `600`, `700`, and `800`.

### Body and interface: Inter

Use for paragraphs, forms, legal text, tables, UI labels, captions, and operational documents.

Approved weights: `400`, `500`, `600`, and `700`.

### Technical accent: IBM Plex Mono

Use sparingly for ticket numbers, service-status labels, technical metadata, small diagrams, timestamps, and short code-like labels. Do not use it for paragraphs or as a generic “cyber” effect.

Approved weights: `400`, `500`, and `600`.

### Editorial accent: Instrument Serif

Use only for occasional campaign pull quotes, thought-leadership covers, or one short phrase in a premium editorial layout. It is not part of the core interface and must never replace the main heading family.

### Fallback stacks

- Display: `"Plus Jakarta Sans", Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`
- Body: `Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`
- Technical: `"IBM Plex Mono", ui-monospace, monospace`
- Editorial: `"Instrument Serif", Georgia, serif`

The package will document font sources and licenses but will not redistribute font files.

## Graphic Language

### Current fields

The primary graphic device is a system of flowing current lines derived from the logo’s rhythm. Current fields may be used as borders, section transitions, low-opacity backgrounds, crop frames, or directional devices. They must remain controlled and geometric rather than becoming decorative ocean illustrations.

### Technical grids

Use subtle grids, node alignments, measured labels, and system diagrams to communicate organization and visibility. Grids must support layout hierarchy; they are not filler.

### Signal points

Small dots, status rings, and short line segments may represent monitoring, uptime, connection, or response. Use them in consistent sizes and with restrained motion.

### Iconography

- Website interface icons may continue to use Lucide as the base library.
- Use consistent `1.75–2 px` strokes at standard interface sizes.
- Do not mix filled clip-art icons with outline icons.
- Service illustrations may combine a Lucide-derived symbol with a current field, but must not place every service inside a shield or glowing card.

### Photography

Preferred photography shows real teams, devices, infrastructure, offices, and South Florida business environments with natural light and believable working conditions.

Avoid:

- Hooded hackers
- Blue-lit server rooms used without context
- Handshakes in empty conference rooms
- Models pointing at transparent screens
- Fake security dashboards
- Tropical stock imagery used only to prove the company is in Florida

When original photography is unavailable, use restrained architectural, infrastructure, or detail imagery with a consistent navy/cyan treatment rather than obviously generated people.

### Motion

- Standard transitions: `180–300 ms`
- Larger section entrances: no more than `500 ms`
- Use subtle line travel, status pulses, and directional movement.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Do not use constant floating cards, excessive glow, particle fields, or animations that imply activity without meaning.

## Layout System

- Base spacing unit: `4 px`
- Standard content rhythm: multiples of `8 px`
- Primary corner radii: `12 px`, `18 px`, and `24 px`
- Full pills are reserved for status labels, compact filters, and tags.
- Desktop website content max width: `1280 px`
- Long-form reading width: `720–780 px`
- Use a 12-column desktop grid and a 4-column mobile grid.
- Use thin Mist Gray dividers on light surfaces and low-opacity white dividers on dark surfaces.
- Shadows remain soft and low-opacity; cyan or green glow is reserved for active signal moments, not every card.
- Headlines should normally occupy no more than three lines on desktop and four lines on mobile.

## Accessibility Requirements

- All normal text must meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Current Navy on Cloud White and Cloud White on Current Navy are default high-contrast combinations.
- Slate on Cloud White is approved for body copy.
- Tide Blue on Cloud White is permitted for sufficiently sized text and interactive labels; smaller text must use a darker link treatment.
- Signal Cyan and Continuity Green are accent fills and dark-background highlights, not default body-copy colors.
- Logos and decorative patterns must not be the sole carrier of meaning.
- Social templates must maintain readable type at phone-preview size.
- Website SVGs require accessible labels when meaningful and `aria-hidden` when decorative.
- Motion and animated accents must have reduced-motion behavior.

## Production Architecture

The package will be reproducible rather than assembled through undocumented manual exports.

- Brand tokens and canonical copy live in one source module.
- Logo SVGs are generated from one canonical path set and lockup specification.
- PNG exports are rendered from the SVG masters at explicit dimensions.
- Social and document graphics use reusable layout functions and safe-area constants.
- PDF brand-guide pages are generated from the same assets used in the package.
- A package manifest records every required output, dimensions, format, and checksum.
- Build scripts fail when an expected file is missing, has the wrong dimensions, or contains a legacy color outside an approved compatibility file.

## Migration Strategy

1. Build and approve the master identity assets before changing the live site.
2. Generate the complete package from the approved master.
3. Integrate the assets and tokens into a review branch.
4. Render the website at mobile, tablet, desktop, light, and dark brand surfaces.
5. Compare old and new recognition at favicon, navigation, proposal-cover, and social-avatar sizes.
6. Replace legacy assets only after the new package and website preview pass verification.
7. Keep the previous logo ZIP in a clearly labeled archive outside the public web root during the transition.
8. Deploy to preview, review, then promote to production.

## Implementation Stages

The work will be planned and executed as five gated stages:

1. **Identity master:** canonical logo geometry, wordmark, color tokens, typography, and approval preview
2. **Digital system:** website components, icons, patterns, Open Graph assets, and favicons
3. **Communication system:** social templates, email/support assets, and document/presentation templates
4. **Guide and packaging:** brand guide, quick-use guide, preview sheet, manifest, and ZIP
5. **Website migration and QA:** token migration, asset replacement, full verification, preview deployment, and production promotion

Each stage must consume the approved output of the prior stage. No downstream template may invent a new logo, color, type treatment, or graphic rule.
