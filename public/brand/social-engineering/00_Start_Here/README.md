## Editing

All production SVG logo lettering is outlined, so the logo never changes because a font is missing. SVG shapes remain editable in Figma, Illustrator, Affinity Designer, and Inkscape. Live-text companions are supplied separately; install Plus Jakarta Sans first. Their characters have explicit positions to preserve the same custom spacing as the production master. Reset positions before changing the wording.

The font families are selected, licensed typefaces, not newly invented GroundSwell font software. Their original binaries and full SIL Open Font License notices are included. The wordmark is custom-set from Plus Jakarta Sans 700; the GS starts with its 800-weight letterforms and integrates the inherited crest. One-color masters express the crest as transparent negative space. The micro GS deliberately removes fine detail at 16 px.

Social templates are exact-size editable vector compositions with outlined copy. Use the bundled fonts and text style references to replace copy. Keep every important element comfortably inside its artboard; the story keeps key text away from the top and bottom controls. The portrait layout keeps the principal headline within the central square area.

## Print

The business card SVGs include a 3.5 × 2 inch trim area with 0.125 inch bleed on all sides. Their 675 × 405 unit artboards use 180 units per inch. Trim: x=22.5, y=22.5, width=630, height=360. Keep essential elements at least 0.125 inch inside the trim. Export using the intended printer's profile and proof before production.

All colors are sRGB digital masters. Use the print provider's intended ICC profile for CMYK conversion. US Letter letterhead is 8.5 × 11 inches; the proposal cover uses the same ratio. Print SVGs are editable production artwork, not PDF/X files with an output intent.
