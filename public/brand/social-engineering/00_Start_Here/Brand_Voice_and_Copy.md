# GroundSwell — voice and copy

## Proposed brand promise
GroundSwell connects brand, social, websites, and marketing to build lasting momentum.

## Core lines
- Brand name: GroundSwell
- Tagline: Built to move people.
- Descriptor: Brand. Social. Digital.
- Endorsement: by New Wave
- Supporting line: Ideas into momentum.

## Short bio
Brand, social, and digital, working together. GroundSwell brings clear ideas and connected execution to your next stage of growth. By New Wave.

## Website introduction
Your brand should feel connected everywhere people find you. GroundSwell brings identity, social content, websites, and marketing into one clear direction, then uses data to keep improving what comes next.

## Voice
Direct, human, informed, and energetic. Start with what people need, then explain the work that will help. Prefer a specific next step to a vague promise. Describe evidence as evidence, estimates as estimates, and ambition as ambition.

## Examples
- Use: Make your presence felt.
- Use: A clearer brand. A stronger next move.
- Use: Turn a good idea into a connected campaign.
- Avoid unsupported promises such as guaranteed virality or fixed growth percentages.

## Existing division language
The current New Wave Social Engineering site uses “Data · Integrations · Smart advertising” and “Growth decisions made on data, not guesswork.” These are reference points for continuity, not requirements to place every line in a logo.
