# Print preparation

Business card: 3.5 × 2 inch trim; 0.125 inch bleed on every edge. Artboard: 3.75 × 2.25 inches (675 × 405 vector units). Trim x=22.5, y=22.5, width=630, height=360. Minimum quiet area inside trim: 22.5 units. Both sides use the same origin; do not rescale one side independently.

Letterhead and proposal cover: US Letter, 8.5 × 11 inches (1224 × 1584 vector units). Letterhead's main writing area is intentionally blank. Use it as stationery artwork behind an editable document.

All master colors use sRGB. Ask the print provider for its required CMYK profile, bleed, and PDF/X export preset. Convert a duplicate, keep the supplied SVG masters unchanged, and inspect a proof. The logos are vectors; avoid rasterizing them for signage or large print.
